Let’s keep the costs clear

Your project.
Your accounts.

Simple brochure sites can be built as static files, with no app server or database to maintain. You own your domain and hosting accounts.

If a project needs a backend, database, paid API or ongoing support, we agree on who runs it and what it costs before building. Monthly care is a separate, optional agreement, not a hidden commitment.

Third-party fees and hosting limits depend on the provider. Existing sites are reviewed for fit before a redesign is quoted.

A few good questions.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the pages, content, features and existing site. We’ll agree on a written scope and quote before any work starts. No surprise extras.

Can you improve my current site?

Yes, if the platform and source access allow it. We’ll review what you have first and recommend a refresh or a rebuild based on the actual work involved.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

Not for every project. A static site can be handed over to your own hosting account. Ongoing updates, monitoring or a hosted backend need their own agreed support and cost arrangement.

Will local SEO put me first on Google?

No one can responsibly promise a ranking. The service focuses on accurate business information, useful local content and technical foundations. Results depend on competition, location and other factors.

Can you build a large custom platform?

Hatsa Web’s custom service starts small: a focused tool, prototype or simple workflow. Larger platforms, regulated systems and complex backends need a separate feasibility review and maintenance plan.
