Small business
deserves good design.

You shouldn’t need a giant project to get a website that explains what you do, looks good on a phone and makes it easy for customers to get in touch.

That’s the thinking behind Hatsa Web. Start with the business, make a clear plan, and build something that serves a real purpose.

It’s also why I’m building Hatsa CRM: to bring the everyday work of a small business into a more organized workspace. The service business and the software product are connected by the same idea: useful technology should feel approachable.
