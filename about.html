I’m Mako, a family man, a one-person business owner and someone who genuinely loves to build. Hatsa Web is where that passion meets a simple belief: small businesses deserve a fair chance to grow.

One small business, helping another

Good tools shouldn’t
feel out of reach.

I started Hatsa Web because building is my passion. But I also know what it’s like to run a small business on your own. You need people to find you, tools that save you time and a website you’re proud to share, without a price tag that puts those things out of reach.

That’s what I want to help with: thoughtful websites, better online visibility and useful software at a fair price. I believe access to tools that help a business grow shouldn’t be reserved for companies with big budgets.

As a family man, I care about the relationships behind the work. I want clients to feel treated like family: listened to, respected and comfortable asking questions. You’re not just another project in a queue.

When you support my business, you help me grow mine. In return, I get to put my skills into something that helps you build yours. That mutual support is the kind of business I want Hatsa Web to be.

I’m not here to sell you more than you need. I’m here to understand what would help, be honest about the scope and costs, and build something useful. Helping others and being fair is the business owner I strive to be.

Rooted in Guam. Built with purpose.

The meaning
behind Håtsa.

I’m Chamorro, from Guam. In my language, håtsa means to lift, raise, or build. That connection means a lot to me.

Building is my passion, and helping other small-business owners move forward is the purpose behind Hatsa Web. To me, the name brings those two things together: building something useful and helping lift others up along the way.
